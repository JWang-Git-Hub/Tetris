GAME MODIFICATIONS & RULES

  • Bomb Blocks: Randomly appears as the next piece. Clears a 3x3 area when placed
  • Power-ups: Earn power-ups by clearing multiple lines at once
  • Line Clear Power-up: Manually clear any completed line (limited uses)
  • Scoring: +100 per line, +300 for 2 lines, +500 for 3 lines, +800 for Tetris (4 lines)
  • Level Up: Every 10 lines increases level and speed
  • Special: Bomb blocks give +500 points and count as 2 lines cleared

Clear as many lines as possible before the blocks reach the top!
